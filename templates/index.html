Good Citizen Waste Habits

Simple daily rules that protect sanitation workers and preserve recyclables.

🧼 The 5-Second Rinse

Leftover food residue breeds bacteria and ruins whole bales of cardboard and plastic. A quick 5-second rinse with dishwater makes packaging genuinely recyclable.

⚠️ Never Trash Loose Batteries

Lithium cells get crushed inside compactor trucks, causing sudden severe fires. Always tape loose battery terminals and take them to supermarket drop-offs.

📦 Flatten All Delivery Boxes

Unbroken cartons waste up to 70% of collection truck volume with empty air. Flattening boxes saves truck fuel and prevents bin overflow.

🛢️ Keep Grease Out of Plumbed Drains

Hot cooking oil hardens inside city pipes into giant fatbergs that choke municipal sewer systems. Pour cooled oil into a sealed bottle for biodiesel pickup.

Documentation & Scientific Honesty Statement

EcoSync strictly differentiates between Household Self-Reported Actions (items you confirmed and segregated into household bins based on standard item unit weights) and Municipal Facility Receipts (physical materials verified at industrial recovery centers).

Emissions avoided and energy saved are calculated deterministically using documented EPA WARM v15 lifecycle factors (e.g., 1.45 kg CO₂e per kg of clean recycled plastic; 0.52 kg CO₂e per kg of composted food scraps). No metrics or predictions are fabricated.
